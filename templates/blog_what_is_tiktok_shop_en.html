What Is TikTok Shop — And Can You Really Make Money With It?

📅 July 2026 👤 By the Qeerah team ⏱️ 11 min read 🇫🇷 Lire en français
🔎 In short: TikTok Shop is TikTok's built-in e-commerce feature, letting people buy directly inside the app through videos, live streams or a storefront. Launched in 2021, it is now live in around twenty countries and generated $64.3 billion in sales in 2025. Yes, you can make money with it — as an affiliate creator earning commissions, or as a seller — but the reality is two-speed: the median creator earns about $200 a month, while a small minority of top creators and brands generate millions.

In under four years, TikTok Shop went from a quiet test in Southeast Asia to one of the largest social commerce machines in the world. Here is what TikTok Shop actually is, how it works, where it is available, and who the American creators making fortunes on it really are — with figures and sources throughout.

📊 The numbers behind the explosion

TikTok Shop's global GMV (gross merchandise value) reached $64.3 billion in 2025, up 94% year on year across roughly fifteen active markets (ContentGrip, Ringly.io). For perspective: it took Amazon roughly 18 years to reach $100 billion in annual GMV; TikTok Shop is on track to pass that mark in its fourth full year (ContentGrip).

In the United States, TikTok Shop already captured 18.2% of all social commerce sales in 2025 (Ringly.io). Longer-term forecasts even suggest a platform capable of approaching $1 trillion in sales by 2030, which would make it a global top-3 retailer behind Amazon (ContentGrip).

🗓️ The international rollout timeline

TikTok Shop did not launch everywhere at once. It opened market by market, at a pace that accelerated sharply from 2023 onwards.

  • 2021 — First integrated e-commerce tests, including a Shopify pilot partnership that ultimately did not stick (Wikipedia).
  • 25 April 2022 — Official cross-border e-commerce launch in four Southeast Asian countries: Thailand, Vietnam, Malaysia and the Philippines (Wikipedia).
  • November 2022 — Beta testing begins in the United Kingdom, alongside several Southeast Asian markets (Wikipedia).
  • 12 September 2023 — Official US launch — the opening that pushed TikTok Shop into another league (CNN Business).
  • 2024-2025 — Consolidation across Southeast Asia (Singapore, Japan) and first Latin American openings in Mexico and Brazil.
  • 2025-2026 — European wave: the UK, France, Germany, Spain, Italy and Ireland establish themselves as mature markets.
  • 15 June 2026 — Austria, Belgium, the Netherlands and Poland join TikTok Shop Europe, with a new "Sell Across Europe" tool letting a single seller registration cover multiple European markets (Novadata).

🇮🇩 Indonesia, a direct rival to the United States

One of the most striking facts of 2025: in the first half of the year, Indonesia overtook the United States as TikTok Shop's biggest market, with $6 billion in GMV against $5.8 billion for the US (Digital in Asia). Across full-year 2025 the US regained the lead ($15.1B vs $13.1B for Indonesia), but Indonesia remains the platform's second-largest market, and the whole Southeast Asia region doubled its GMV in a year to $45.6 billion (TechNode Global).

💰 The American creators who became millionaires on TikTok Shop

Founder of Canvas Beauty and its Body Glaze line, Stormi Steele became the first creator to pass $1 million in sales during a single TikTok Shop live session, on 8 June 2024 (Ebony). On Black Friday, her brand generated $3 million in sales in a single day, including $2.1 million through a 12-hour "mega live" hosted from her Alabama warehouse (WWD). Canvas Beauty has since been named the #1 selling brand on TikTok Shop in America, on track for $6 million in monthly sales (Blavity, Forbes).

Logan Walter dropped out of college at 21 to focus on TikTok Shop, where he established himself as one of the few male affiliates in the beauty and self-care space, selling brands like Medicube and Neutrogena. His first viral video earned him over $20,000 in a month; two years after starting, he was pulling a seven-figure monthly income (Fortune).

Katrina Dimiele — Live fashion selling (try-on livestreams)

A fashion creator specialising in live "try-on hauls", Katrina Dimiele has accumulated $40 million in sales over nine years through her live streams on TikTok Shop and Facebook (video interview, TikTok Shop Millionaire). Her path illustrates something important: most of the biggest wins come from creators who accumulated thousands of hours of practice before breaking out, not from a single lucky moment.

🔑 Key takeaway: all three paths share one thing — live shopping. It is the format that produces the most spectacular revenue spikes on TikTok Shop, far more than short videos alone.

📉 What about a "normal" creator?

These stories are real, but they are not the average. According to 2026 monetisation data, the median TikTok creator earns roughly $200 per month across all income sources, while the top 10% combine multiple revenue streams to reach $2,000–$5,000 per month (Branvas). The millions earned by Stormi Steele, Logan Walter or Katrina Dimiele are the visible peak of a far broader pyramid.

🔮 What comes next

The expansion is not slowing: after the European wave of June 2026, TikTok Shop is already testing paid subscriptions that echo the Amazon Prime model (Startup Fortune), and established retailers such as Ulta Beauty have joined the platform in the US, betting on its ability to turn discovery into purchase (Fortune).

❓ Frequently asked questions

What is TikTok Shop?

TikTok Shop is TikTok's built-in e-commerce feature, letting users buy products directly inside the app through short videos, live shopping streams or a storefront. Launched in 2021, it is live in around twenty countries as of mid-2026, including the United States, the United Kingdom, France, Germany, Spain, Italy, Ireland, Indonesia, Thailand, Vietnam, Malaysia, the Philippines, Singapore, Japan, Mexico and Brazil.

Can you really make money on TikTok Shop?

Yes. Creators earn affiliate commissions by recommending products, and sellers can run their own storefront. Creators such as Stormi Steele (Canvas Beauty) and Logan Walter became millionaires through TikTok Shop. But results are highly uneven: the median TikTok creator earns roughly $200 per month, and only a small minority reach seven-figure income.

Which countries is TikTok Shop available in?

As of mid-2026 TikTok Shop is live in around twenty markets: the Americas (United States, Mexico, Brazil), Europe (United Kingdom, France, Germany, Spain, Italy, Ireland, plus Austria, Belgium, the Netherlands and Poland since June 2026), and Asia-Pacific (Indonesia, Thailand, Vietnam, Malaysia, the Philippines, Singapore, Japan). Australia and the Gulf states have no official launch yet.
